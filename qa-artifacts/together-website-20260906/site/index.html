Questions people actually ask

Lantern Pages is intentionally low-pressure. Here is what first-time readers usually want to know.

Do I have to finish the book?

No. Bring a favorite passage, a question, or simply an interest in listening. We announce spoilers before discussing the ending.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There are no dues or required purchases, and library copies are always encouraged.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes, and you do not need to tell us first. First-time visitors are always welcome.

What if I have never come before?

Arrive a few minutes early and look for the paper lantern. Someone will offer a reading schedule and introduce you around.

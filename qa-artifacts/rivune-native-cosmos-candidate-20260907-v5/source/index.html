How Council works

Different perspectives. One considered response.

In Council mode, multiple AIs answer your request independently. A mediator reviews their contributions and brings them into one response you can question, refine, and build on.

  1. 01

    Ask once

    Start one conversation with the question or task you are working through.

  2. 02

    Compare perspectives

    Connected AI tools respond independently, so useful differences stay visible.

  3. 03

    Shape the response

    A mediator brings the strongest contributions into a draft that remains yours to review.

Council is in active development. Rivune helps organize perspectives; it does not guarantee that an AI response is correct.

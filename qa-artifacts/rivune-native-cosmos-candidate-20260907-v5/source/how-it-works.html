How it works

Choose how your AI tools work.

Use one provider directly, compare independent perspectives, or coordinate separate tasks. Availability is shown for each strategy.

Normal AIFirst Tauri milestone · not verified+

The planned first Tauri workflow sends a conversation to one selected provider. No live Tauri provider request has been verified yet.

  1. Choose a connected provider.
  2. Write one request.
  3. Review and continue the conversation.
Council strategyLegacy Mac development only+

Multiple AIs answer independently. A mediator reviews the successful contributions and brings them into one response for you to question and refine.

  1. Choose the participating providers.
  2. Keep their perspectives independent.
  3. Review the mediator’s combined draft.

Council exists in later legacy Mac development, but its Tauri port and runtime verification remain pending.

Swarm strategyTauri port pending+

An appointed lead divides a request into bounded subtasks, delegates them, reviews the returned work, and integrates the result.

  1. The lead plans separate tasks.
  2. Team members complete assigned work.
  3. The lead reviews and integrates the output.

Swarm remains a planned Tauri workflow. It has not been integrated or verified in the Tauri runtime.

The Tauri desktop app is in development for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Downloads are not yet available. Normal AI is the first runtime milestone; Council and Swarm ports remain pending.

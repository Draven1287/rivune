Why I built Rivune.

Different models have different strengths, and I was curious about what they could do together. That’s why I built Rivune.

Constellation is being built to bring multiple AI perspectives and shared project work into one conversation, with a lead reviewing the contributions and bringing them together—for coding and everyday tasks.

If you have a question, find a bug, have a concern, or have an idea for improving Rivune, I’d love to hear from you.

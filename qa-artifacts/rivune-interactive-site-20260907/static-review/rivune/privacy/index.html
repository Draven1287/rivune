Privacy & data sharing

Your history is local.
AI processing uses providers.

This describes the legacy Mac source preview, the planned Tauri desktop app, and this project website. No verified Tauri download or live provider integration is available yet.

Conversation history

The legacy Mac build stores conversation history locally by default. Local storage does not mean AI processing stays on your computer. Tauri storage and routing must be verified before release.

What providers receive

In the legacy Mac build, Direct mode sends your request to the selected provider. The earlier Together workflow shares the relevant prompt, included context, attached text, plans, contributions, and reviews with OpenAI and Anthropic. Earlier Together runs remain Together; they are not Council or Swarm. Review what you include before sending.

Provider terms govern their processing, retention, limits, and charges. Rivune does not promise retention-free processing. Multiple-model work can use more provider requests than a single answer.

Council and Swarm: planned data sharing

The legacy public source preview 4 predates the configurable team experience, and those behaviors have not yet been ported to Tauri. Council is a planned workflow: it is designed to send the same approved context to independent participants, then give their answers to the proposed or user-selected manager. Swarm is designed to share approved project context with the agents assigned to separate tasks, then review and integrate their work.

These are proposed data paths, not a claim that the public source release includes them. Their controls and actual execution must be verified before release. The Council vs. Swarm guide explains the planned workflows and team/manager choice.

Accounts and connections

In the legacy Mac work, provider sign-in or your own API keys provide model access, subject to the provider’s terms. No live Tauri provider connection has been verified. A Rivune account is separate and does not grant model access. Account services are disabled in the public source export until an operator configures a supported backend. This static website has no account service or cloud conversation sync.

This website

This site contains product information and release links. It does not run AI, inspect installed tools, ask for API keys, or collect waitlist submissions. No analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or third-party font requests are included.

The hosting provider receives ordinary web requests, such as an IP address and browser information. When published on GitHub Pages, GitHub’s privacy statement applies to hosting. Downloads, source browsing, and support links take you to GitHub.

Questions or a security concern?

The Email Aarav link opens your email app. Your message is addressed to a public alias and passes through an email forwarding service; this website has no contact form. Email delivery depends on that service.

You can also ask a question on GitHub. Do not include private prompts or credentials in a public issue. For security vulnerabilities, follow the security reporting instructions.

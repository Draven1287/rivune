Before you get started

Your Mac.
Your provider access.

Rivune is the workspace. The AI models come from the providers you connect.

Provider subscriptions, permissions, usage limits, and charges apply separately.

  • A compatible Mac

    {{MAC_REQUIREMENT}}

  • A supported provider connection

    The current source preview supports Codex CLI and Claude Code CLI. Install the tool you want to use and complete that provider’s sign-in. Rivune Settings checks whether it is ready.

  • Access to the models you choose

    Your provider must allow access to the selected model. A free account may not include it. Using Rivune does not grant access or include AI usage.

  • Workflow availability

    Source preview 4 predates the one-team experience. Council exists only in later local development; real Swarm and Auto are not public release features. Connecting a provider does not enable them.

Questions, answered

What to know before you try Rivune.

Can I download the Mac app now?

The Mac installer is coming soon. Developer source preview 4 is available now, requires Xcode to build, and is not a signed installer or DMG.

Does Rivune replace my provider account?

No. Rivune is a separate workspace. You connect a supported provider and sign in with that provider’s own tools.

Does Rivune include AI usage?

No. Model access, subscriptions, usage limits, and charges come from the providers you choose.

Where do conversations and requests go?

Conversation history is stored locally by default. Requests go to the connected providers used in the conversation, and a lead-directed team may share approved context or successful member answers. Read privacy & data sharing for exact routing.

Meet the developer

Built from a question about what AI tools could do together.

Hi, I’m Aarav, a 15-year-old high school student based in Denver, Colorado. I wanted one app for the AI tools I use every day.

Different models have different strengths, and I was curious about what they could do together. That’s why I built Rivune.

Its Council mode is designed to let multiple AIs answer independently, then have a mediator review and combine their contributions into one response—for coding and everyday tasks.

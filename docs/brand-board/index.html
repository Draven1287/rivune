Our direction

Codex-inspired clarity and efficiency. Rivune’s own galaxy identity. Constellation Engine brings multiple AI perspectives into useful results.

Who we are building for

Professionals doing demanding work at companies such as Nvidia, Apple, Tesla and SpaceX. These describe our intended audience, not existing customers.

What the experience must deliver

Readable conversations, predictable controls, responsive workflows and dependable recovery. The galaxy adds character; the work stays easy to follow.

Efficiency is a target to verify

Latest decision: Traycer is no longer a design reference. Implementation remains paused while we define Rivune together.

# Revised Rivune offer direction

September7. User asked root to decide and wants all access paths. This supersedes the earlier sync-only10USD recommendation as product direction for validation, not live checkout or a promise that cloud features exist. Keep previous pricing research and scenarios intact.

## Updated target after user requested a higher price

The user asked to make Plus more expensive to build a profitable business. Revised target: **19USD/month**, superseding the12USD target below. Keep the free local core and separately metered optional hosted AI. Plus must deliver reliable sync, versioned backup/restore, and away-from-home phone access while the user's Mac is awake/reachable before charging. This target is not a live offer or evidence of willingness to pay. At100 paying monthly users gross subscription revenue is1900USD/month before costs, refunds and taxes. More revenue requires paying retained customers; price alone does not establish profit.

At the existing illustrative50-subscriber cost assumptions (3.6% plus0.30 payment/Billing fees,0.50 backend allocation,0.4926 storage/egress shadow and2.00 reserve),19 leaves15.0234USD per subscriber before omitted expenses. At10subscribers,2.50 backend allocation lowers that to13.0234USD. These are scenarios, not measured margins; support/relay usage can exceed the reserve. Validate the complete bundle and customer demand in a pilot before public pricing. Consider190USD/year only after retention and cost evidence; the old120 annual suggestion is superseded. No cloud usage is bundled or unlimited.

This update changes planning only: no paid infrastructure, checkout, public site pricing, outreach or purchase.

## Prior decision retained for comparison

One Rivune product, free local core, one optional paid convenience plan, optional independently purchasable hosted AI usage. No separate Council/Swarm subscriptions.

- Rivune Free: local workspace, projects, own supported provider accounts/keys, lead-directed Council/Swarm when verified, export and same-LAN phone control when verified. Provider access/charges remain the user's. No artificial local message quota; real provider/runtime safety and work limits remain.
- Rivune Plus: target12USD/month for synchronized workspace, versioned cloud backup/restore, and secure away-from-home phone access. Cross-device continuity and recovery are the paid benefit. Relay-to-Mac execution requires the Mac awake and reachable; this is not Mac-off hosted execution. Bounded storage/retention and relay usage must be specified and measured before sale. Do not charge until the advertised bundle works.
- Cloud AI balance: later optional10USD minimum top-up, usable without Plus. Dollars debited at published task/model rates with estimate and maximum before execution. No automatic recharge by default. A balance is not a promise of a fixed number of answers or equivalent raw provider spend. Actual markup/rates remain unset until real per-run provider+orchestration costs are measured; the earlier5x scenario is not approved pricing. No unlimited frontier-AI bundle.

Start with free local release and developer-led pilot, then a monthly Plus pilot after verified sync/restore/relay and demand. Do not force early users into an annual commitment. Consider120USD/year (two monthly payments saved) only after retention and variable costs are known; do not offer lifetime cloud obligations. A later team plan requires actual collaboration demand and features, not a fourth launch tier.

## Why12 instead of8 or9

We are testing a complete continuity/recovery benefit rather than a discount against Traycer's10USD sync. At50 illustrative subscribers and baseline2GBstorage/5GBegress,12 leaves8.2754USD after assumed domestic-card/Billing fees, allocated backend, full-volume storage/egress shadow, and an illustrative2USDreserve.8 leaves4.4194USD;9 leaves5.3834USD. These are not profits or measured margins. The reserve may be inadequate, and tax/channel/engineering/high-usage costs remain. At10subscribers the12case remainder drops to6.2754USD. See qa-artifacts/pricing-20260907/refined-offer. Price depends on perceived value; if users will not pay for the bundle, refine the benefit instead of claiming arithmetic proves demand.

An annual120 payment yields9.615USD monthly equivalent after the assumed single payment/Billing fee, before infrastructure/reserve. Annual cash is twelve months of service obligation, not immediate profit.

## Experience rules

Free mode must remain useful. Plus does not unlock safety, trustworthy output or basic file recovery locally. No paid wall on exporting existing local data. Provider connection, Plus subscription and hosted balance are separate internal entitlements but should be understandable in one account screen. Never charge for a model call made under the user's own provider account as though Rivune supplied it. Show real billing source before work. Do not promise cloud execution while the Mac is off as part of remote access.

Public Pages remains project/download information; paid offers need suitable separate hosting and actual service readiness. This decision creates no payment accounts, infrastructure, purchase, public pricing page or launch. Current app/site polish continues ahead of monetization implementation.

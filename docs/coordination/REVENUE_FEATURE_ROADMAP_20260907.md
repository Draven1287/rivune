Rivune helps Mac users coordinate AI work, inspect reviews, and finish useful deliverables. First customer hypothesis: solo freelancers producing client proposals and reports. A focused first workflow supports the broader multi-agent workspace; it does not replace that direction.

Retain the existing free local core and $19/month Plus hypothesis from PRICING_PRODUCT_DECISION_20260907.md. Basic local projects, Council/Swarm when verified, local exports and safety/recovery are not newly paywalled. The earlier $15 suggestion in the initiating conversation is superseded for planning. No separate Council and Swarm subscriptions.

## Pricing hypotheses

| Offer | Proposed test | Paid benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Useful local workspace and existing promised local functionality; user pays their own provider. |
| Plus | $19/month | Verified sync, backup/restore and remote continuity. Existing hypothesis retained. |
| Pro | $39/month, later | Plus with advanced persistent project coordination and recurring/shared workflow capabilities once validated. Exact packaging and limits require pilot evidence. |
| Teams | $59/user/month, later | Pro with actual team collaboration, permissions and approvals; validate willingness to pay and support burden before offering. |

AI usage remains separate. No unlimited hosted inference, transfer of personal subscription credentials to Rivune servers, or unmeasured included credit allowance. A higher price must earn its place through repeated customer value. Model review does not guarantee factual correctness.

At 100 retained Pro subscribers, $39/month is $3,900 gross monthly revenue. This is arithmetic, not a forecast or profit; deduct payment costs, infrastructure, support, refunds, taxes and any model usage Rivune supplies. Do not use a laptop savings goal as evidence of customer value.

Checkout/licensing follows product and demand validation. Acceptance must cover purchase entitlement, cancellation, renewal failure, restore and refunds, with exact advertised limits. This document makes no external purchases, publishes no price, creates no infrastructure and starts no outreach.

## Competitive evidence checked 2026-09-07

Traycer's pricing page lists free BYOA planning/tracking and agent communication, $10 Sync, $20 Lite, $40 Pro and $100 Ultra; its paid inference tiers include credits. The page still displays an August 31 promotion, so promotional availability should be checked at checkout rather than assumed current. Source: https://traycer.ai/pricing

Traycer describes agents working side by side, agent-to-agent handoffs and shared task context. Source: https://traycer.ai/

Inference: copying planning and multi-agent chat alone gives Rivune a weak reason to charge, because Traycer advertises those at $0. Test Rivune's reviewed deliverable and continuity experience; do not equate Traycer's credit-inclusive prices with a Rivune software-only margin.

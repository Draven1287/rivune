FAQ

What to know before you try Rivune.

Availability, provider access, AI usage, and data routing—without hidden assumptions.

Can I download the Mac app now?

The Mac installer is coming soon. Developer source preview 4 is available now, requires Xcode, and is not a signed installer or DMG. See availability →

Does Rivune replace my provider account?

No. Rivune is a separate workspace. You connect a supported provider and sign in with that provider’s own tools.

Does Rivune include AI usage?

No. Model access, subscriptions, usage limits, and charges come from the providers you choose.

Where do conversations and requests go?

Conversation history is local by default. Requests go to providers used in the conversation, and lead-directed teamwork may share approved context or successful member answers. Read exact routing →

Before you get started

Your Mac.
Your provider access.

Rivune is the workspace. The AI models come from the providers you connect.

Provider subscriptions, permissions, usage limits, and charges apply separately.

  • A compatible Mac

    {{MAC_REQUIREMENT}}

  • A supported provider connection

    The source preview supports Codex CLI and Claude Code CLI. Install the tool you use and complete that provider’s sign-in.

  • Access to selected models

    Your provider must allow access to the model. Rivune does not grant model access or include AI usage.

  • Current workflow limits

    Source preview 4 predates the one-team experience. Configurable Council is limited to later local development; real Swarm and Auto are not public release features.
